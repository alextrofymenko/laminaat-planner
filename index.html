How to Use

Drawing a Room

  • Click on canvas to place vertices
  • Click near first point to close the room
  • Backspace to undo last vertex while drawing
  • Escape to cancel drawing

Editing Room Shape

  • Click wall label on canvas to select and edit dimension
  • First dimension edit scales room proportionally
  • Shift + Drag vertex to move it
  • Shift + Click vertex to delete it
  • Shift + Click wall to add a vertex

Adjusting Floor Layout

  • Right-click + Drag (or Ctrl+Drag) to move floor position
  • Rotation slider to angle the planks
  • Click a plank to see details and adjust row offset

Navigation

  • Scroll wheel to zoom in/out
  • Click + Drag to pan the view
  • Click plank preview to see enlarged detail
